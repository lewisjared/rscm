Added unit conveniences:

- `Model.coupling()` lists each unit conversion with its direction and the units it converts between.
- `Timeseries`, `FourBoxTimeseries` and `HemisphericTimeseries` gain `convert_to(unit)`, returning a copy in another compatible unit.
- `with_initial_values` and `with_initial_grid_values` accept a `(value, unit)` pair, converted into the unit the model stores the variable in.
