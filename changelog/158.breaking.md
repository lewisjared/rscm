Reworked MAGICC component interfaces to match MAGICC7.

- `CO2Budget` no longer takes `Emissions|CO2|Land Use`, because `Carbon Flux|Terrestrial` already includes land-use emissions. It takes `Emissions|CO2|CH4 Oxidation` instead.
- `OceanCarbon` takes `Surface Temperature`, fossil emissions, CH4 oxidation and the land flux instead of `Sea Surface Temperature`, so that atmospheric CO2 can evolve within the year. `pco2_pi` now defaults to the initial ocean pCO2.
- `CH4Chemistry` replaces `ch4_pi`, `tau_oh` and the reference emissions with `feedback_start_year`, `tau_total_init` and `mixbox_size`. Its defaults are now the MAGICC7 values.
- `N2OChemistry` replaces `n2o_pi` with `feedback_start_year` and requires `Surface Temperature` for the meridional flux feedback.
- `run_magicc` defaults the climate `rf_2xco2` to the CO2-doubling ERF of the forcing configuration, about 3.9 W/m² for OLBL.
