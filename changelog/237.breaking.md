Replaced TOML model serialisation with JSON `Record`s.
`Model.to_toml`, `Model.from_toml` and Rust `Model::from_checkpoint` were removed, and `Model` no longer implements `Serialize` or `Deserialize`.
Checkpoints saved by earlier versions cannot be read.
Pickle and `copy.deepcopy` of a `Model` now go through a record.
A record of another format is an error, and one from another rscm version restores with a warning.
Stepping a model that has components but no step plan now fails instead of advancing without solving.
