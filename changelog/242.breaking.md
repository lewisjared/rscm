`TimeseriesCollection.get_timeseries_by_name`, `get_four_box_timeseries_by_name` and `get_hemispheric_timeseries_by_name` raise `TypeError` for a variable on another grid instead of returning `None`. Index the collection, `collection[name]`, to get a series on any grid.

The grids' `region_names()` use MAGICC7's output labels, which `rscm.interop` writes: `World` for the scalar grid, `World|Northern Hemisphere` and `World|Southern Hemisphere` for the hemispheric grid, and `World|Northern Hemisphere|Ocean`, `World|Northern Hemisphere|Land`, `World|Southern Hemisphere|Ocean` and `World|Southern Hemisphere|Land` for the four-box grid.
