Added `rscm.interop`, which converts timeseries on every grid to and from pandas and xarray, see [Pandas and xarray](interop.md).

- `TimeseriesCollection.to_dataframe()` returns a wide DataFrame indexed by `variable`, `unit` and `region`, and `to_xarray()` an xarray Dataset. `TimeseriesCollection.from_dataframe` and `from_xarray` read them back, keeping the time axis, interpolation strategies and grid weights.
- `rscm.interop.to_inputs` and `from_inputs` convert a DataFrame or collection to and from the `(values, unit)` inputs of `rscm.magicc` and `rscm.fair` `setup_model`.
- `FourBoxTimeseries` and `HemisphericTimeseries` gained `global_mean()`, `grid`, `interpolation_strategy`, `interpolate_into()` and a `grid` keyword to set their weights. `Timeseries` gained `interpolate_into()`, and `Timeseries.from_values` takes a `unit`.
- `TimeseriesCollection` iterates over the names of the series on every grid.
- xarray is installed with the new `xarray` extra.
