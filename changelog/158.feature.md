Added `rscm.magicc.run_magicc_emissions` and `Emissions` for emissions-driven runs that calculate CO2, CH4 and N2O with the coupled carbon cycle and chemistry. Temperature feeds back on the land and ocean sinks and on the CH4 and N2O lifetimes. Against MAGICC7 v7.5.3 SSP245 run emissions-driven from 1750, concentrations stay within 0.5 ppm CO2 and 3 ppb CH4, and temperature within 0.02 K. Also added `CH4OxidationBuilder`, which adds the CO2 released by oxidation of fossil CH4.
